PRIVACY POLICY

Qi Jianghu (the "Game")

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Game collects, uses, stores, and
protects information when you play. It applies to the Android app and
any companion web build distributed by the developer. By installing
or using the Game, you agree to the practices described below.

1. WHO WE ARE

The Game is developed and published by an independent developer
("we", "us", "our"). If you have questions about this policy or how
your data is handled, you can contact us using the support details
listed on the app's store page.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We aim to collect the minimum data necessary to run the Game. The
categories of information involved are:

  a) Game progress and system data (stored locally on your device)
     - Character stats, inventory, equipment, currency, cultivation
       progress, quest/path progress, settings (e.g. volume), and
       other save-game data generated as you play.
     - This data is stored locally on your device using browser/app
       storage (e.g. localStorage) and is not transmitted to our
       servers unless you choose to sign in (see below).

  b) Account and authentication data (only if you sign in)
     - If you choose to link or create an account using "Sign in
       with Google" or "Sign in with Apple", we use Firebase
       Authentication (a Google service) to manage that sign-in.
     - This may involve transmitting and storing a unique account
       identifier, your display name, email address, and profile
       picture (as provided by Google/Apple), to Firebase's servers
       so that your identity can be verified and, where applicable,
       your progress can be associated with your account.
     - We do not receive or store your Google/Apple account
       password. Authentication is handled entirely by Google/Apple
       and Firebase.

  c) Technical and diagnostic data
     - Basic device and app information (such as app version,
       platform, and crash/error logs) may be generated to help
       diagnose problems. Where this is transmitted off-device, it
       is sent via Firebase infrastructure.

We do not collect payment card details, precise location data, or
contacts. The Game does not contain third-party advertising SDKs.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the information described above only to:
  - Save and restore your game progress.
  - Authenticate you if you choose to sign in.
  - Diagnose and fix technical issues (crash/error data).
  - Maintain the security and integrity of the Game.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your data for targeted
advertising.

4. TRANSMISSION TO AND STORAGE BY FIREBASE

Where applicable, data described in Section 2(b) and 2(c) is
transmitted to and processed by Firebase, a cloud platform operated
by Google. Firebase may store this data on servers located outside
of the United Kingdom or European Economic Area, including in the
United States. Google provides contractual and technical safeguards
(such as Standard Contractual Clauses) for these transfers.

Firebase's handling of this data is additionally governed by
Google's own Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy)
and Firebase's data processing terms. We encourage you to review
these if you want full details of Google's own practices as a
data processor/sub-processor.

Game progress data stored only on your device (Section 2(a)) is not
sent to Firebase unless a future feature (such as cloud save) is
enabled and clearly communicated to you.

5. DATA RETENTION

  - Locally stored game data remains on your device until you
    clear the app's storage/data or uninstall the Game.
  - Account data held in Firebase Authentication is retained for as
    long as your account exists, or until you request deletion.

6. YOUR RIGHTS

If you are in the UK or EEA, you have rights under the UK GDPR / EU
GDPR, including the right to:
  - Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  - Request correction or deletion of your personal data.
  - Withdraw consent for sign-in based features (by signing out
    and/or deleting your account).
  - Lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office
    (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you believe your data has been mishandled.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details on the app's
store listing. Since most game data lives on your own device, you
can also delete it at any time via your device's app settings
(clear storage/data or uninstall).

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age
of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect
personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us
with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. DATA SECURITY

We rely on Firebase's built-in security infrastructure (encryption
in transit via HTTPS/TLS, and Google's access controls) to protect
any data transmitted off-device. No method of electronic storage or
transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute
security.

9. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The only third-party service integrated into the Game is Firebase
(Google), used for optional sign-in and diagnostics. The Game does
not integrate third-party analytics or advertising SDKs beyond this.
If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated
accordingly.

10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example to
reflect new features (such as cloud saves) or legal requirements.
The "Last updated" date at the top of this document will reflect the
most recent changes. Continued use of the Game after changes take
effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. CONTACT US

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your
data is handled, please contact us via the support/contact details
provided on the Game's Google Play Store listing.
